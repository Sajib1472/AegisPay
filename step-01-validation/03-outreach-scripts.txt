AegisPay — outreach scripts (Step 1.2)
Do not pitch features. Ask for a de-identified punch export.

------------------------------------------------
Sources, in this order
------------------------------------------------
1. Local dental study clubs, state dental association classifieds, DSO office-manager groups
2. Multi-location PT / outpatient rehab groups (often 4–8 sites, production pay)
3. Veterinary groups that just rolled up 2–6 hospitals
4. CPAs and bookkeepers who already run payroll for 10+ clinics (best channel)

Target: 30 named people in 02-buyer-pipeline.csv. Status values:
  prospect | messaged | call_booked | discovery_done | export_received | autopsy_sent | paid_150 | closed_no

------------------------------------------------
Cold DM / email (office manager)
------------------------------------------------
Subject: The Excel day before payroll

Office managers at multi-location clinics tell me they spend a day before each payroll
fixing overtime, missed lunches, and hygienist bonuses in Excel so Gusto doesn’t underpay people.

I’m building the checker that does that automatically and produces an audit file.

If I ran your last two pay periods for free, would you send me a de-identified punch export?
(Employee codes instead of names is enough. No SSNs, no bank accounts, no patient data.)

------------------------------------------------
Follow-up (5 days later)
------------------------------------------------
Quick bump — happy to do this as a 20-minute payroll autopsy, not a software demo.
You keep Gusto. I only show where the hours were wrong.

------------------------------------------------
CPA / bookkeeper version
------------------------------------------------
Subject: Clinic payroll you already touch

If you have clinic clients whose office managers still rebuild overtime in Excel the day
before payday, I’ll audit two pay periods for free and send you a cited exception list
you can hand the owner.

If it finds money, we split nothing — you look like the hero; I ask the owner for a $299/month tool.
If you prefer to resell it later, we can talk channel pricing after three intros.

------------------------------------------------
What to refuse in the first message
------------------------------------------------
- “We’ll do full payroll and tax filing”
- “HIPAA certified”
- “All 50 states”
- Discounting below $299 before you have 10 customers
