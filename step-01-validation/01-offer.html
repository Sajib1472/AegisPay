The ritual we kill

The day before payday, your office manager exports punches from the time clock, fixes overtime in Excel, hunts for missed lunches, true-up hygienist production bonuses, then uploads a CSV to Gusto. One wrong regular rate and you either short the team or overpay. One missed California meal premium and you have a wage claim.

Punches → overtime → meal premiums → production true-up → Gusto. That whole day becomes a 20-minute review.

1. Punch timeline Every IN, break, and OUT on a calendar day, with late or missing meals marked in red.
2. Exception queue Unpaired punches, missing rates, and generated premiums — blockers vs warnings — before anyone is paid.
3. Payroll-ready register Regular, daily OT, weekly OT, meal/rest premiums, bonus true-up. Click any dollar to see the statute and the math. Export a Gusto file.
